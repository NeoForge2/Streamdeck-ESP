Weather card

Displays an icon based on the current condition (sun, rain, snow, clouds, night, etc.), animated on the screen, together with the temperature. Requires a weather integration configured in Home Assistant, such as OpenWeatherMap, Pirate Weather or another provider exposing a weather.* entity.
